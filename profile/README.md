Aworkado collects job postings from the career pages of thousands of companies (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters and more), keeps only remote and hybrid roles, and checks every one again within a day or two. Each posting links to the employer's own application page. It's free and there is no account to create.

**[aworkado-mcp](https://github.com/aworkado/aworkado-mcp)** is our public MCP server. Connect `https://aworkado.co/mcp` to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or any other MCP client and ask for jobs in plain language, like "remote customer support jobs open to people in India". In Claude the results show up as job cards.
